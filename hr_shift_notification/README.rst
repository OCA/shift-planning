Notify employees when their shift planning changes. A button on the
planning form opens a wizard that lists the shifts to notify; each
selected employee receives the message on their chatter. Employees can
opt out from these notifications.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

1. Open a shift planning. The *To Notify* button counts the shifts that
   have changed since the last notification.
2. Click it to open the wizard, uncheck any employee to skip, and click
   *Send*.

**Opt-out.** From *Preferences*, a user can turn shift notifications off
for themselves. For an employee without a user account, an HR manager
sets the flag on the employee form.
